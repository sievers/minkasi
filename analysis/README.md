# Analysis scripts for the needlet mapmaker

Tests, diagnostics and figures for the joint MUSTANG-2 + ACT needlet maps made with `examples/twores_ex.py`
(`minkasi.needlet.joint.JointMapper`). Run them from this directory: some import functions from each other.
`plot_*_aplpy.py` need `aplpy` and `compare_gif.py` needs `reproject`. `slurm/` holds the A399-401 batch jobs
(dry run, smoke test, full run, post-processing) and a per-node memory sampler.

- `a399_401_fig1.py`: Figure 1 (right) of Hincks et al. 2022 (arXiv:2107.04611) redone with the joint M2 + ACT two-resolution map of A399-401: the 90 GHz multiscale display map (multiscale_map.py, same colour scale) of the joint solve, with their ACT + Planck y-map as contours at 3, 5, ..., 15 sigma, the clusters' R500c circles (their Tables 1 and 4), the ACT compact sources coloured by band and sized by the beam, and the beams. Next to it, cutouts of the M2 region in ACT f090 alone and in the joint map, same pixels and colour scale, to show what M2 adds.
- `act_consistency.py`: Does a joint M2 + ACT map agree with ACT where both measure? The joint map (twores_ex _final.fits) is seen as ACT sees it (ACT beam, normalised to the M2 footprint, then averaged over each ACT pixel) and compared with the ACT coadd in the ACT pixels fully inside the region where M2 has at least --min-hits hits, in units of the ACT noise per pixel. Prints the rms of that difference after removing an offset (1 = consistent with ACT's white noise), its tilt and bowl at the edge of the region, and the rms left after removing them. With --sim-sky the ACT map is simulated from the sky with the real ACT noise, as a check of the method.
- `band_error.py`: Where the simulation's map error lives, per needlet band: the error (map - truth) of saved CG maps split into the bands (component F_i^2 m of band i, of the error inside the footprint; the saved maps are zero outside), measured in the footprint interior and in the edge strip inside the footprint. Also the share of each band in the error of the 16-64 px Gaussian band-pass at the edge (Figure 6 of the summary page), and the prior strength a signal prior would give for the bands the outside prior acts on: eps = 1 / (sky variance per pixel of the sum of those bands), from the simulated sky components.
- `bowl_profiles.py`: Radial profiles around the cluster, to see the M2 bowl and how much of it the joint map keeps. All maps are seen at ACT resolution (ACT beam, 30" pixels) inside the M2 region, with each map's tilt over the region removed and zero at its outer edge. Top row: real data, against ACT. Bottom row: the simulation, against the truth (which shows that the method recovers the cluster and does not make a bowl). Right column: the cluster profile itself at --fine-fwhm resolution, real joint map and simulated joint map against the simulated truth.
- `bowling_test.py`: Test whether large scale "bowling" (radial gradient / saddle shape) builds up with CG iterations. A quadratic surface is fitted to each saved map (maps of twores_ex.py --cg-save) in the footprint, outside the central cluster, and the amplitude of the fitted surface is followed against the iteration for each run, e.g. with and without ACT. Also shows the maps smoothed to a few arcmin.
- `cg_convergence.py`: Plot how much the needlet CG map still changes between saved iterations, per angular scale, relative to the rms of the last saved map in that band (maps saved by twores_ex.py --cg-save).
- `compare_gif.py`: Blink GIF (and optionally a side by side PNG) comparing the final direct needlet map with the maps of minkasi_mpi_example.py (plain PCG) and minkasi_mask_example.py (hits-masked PCG). The PCG maps are reprojected onto the grid of the needlet map and every frame shows only the pixels that are good in all three, on a shared colour scale.
- `compare_maps_bands.py`: Compare final maps band by band: rms of each map and of its difference with the first map in Gaussian band-passes, plus the central decrement, e.g. the ACT weight variants of twores_ex.py.
- `make_sim_sky.py`: Make a simulated sky for testing twores_ex.py (--sim-sky): the cluster and point source of a WITCH model (beam convolved, in the M2 data units, K_RJ) plus a lensed CMB realisation at the M2 beam, written as a FITS image with a CAR WCS centred on the model. Extensions hold the components. This script uses WITCH; twores_ex.py only reads the FITS file.
- `multiscale_map.py`: Two-resolution display map (for visualization only, not for science). The base grid is the ACT pixelization. Every ACT pixel containing M2 data is split into factor x factor sub-pixels (factor ideally 2^n). Sub-pixels with enough M2 hits show the M2 + ACT joint map, the others keep their ACT pixel's value. ACT pixels without M2 data show the ACT map. The two regions share one colour scale; the joint map's offset can be matched to ACT where they overlap, the M2 part lightly smoothed, and the joint map feathered into ACT by hits so that the noisy low-hit edge fades into ACT.
- `needlet_benchmark.py`: Benchmark and accuracy check of the probed (sparse) ANA against the exact one, with figures for reports/needlet.html.
- `plot_final_aplpy.py`: Plot the final M2 map, the final map at ACT resolution on the ACT scale and the ACT map with aplpy (sky coordinates, colour bars in Jy/beam). With --compare, plot a second final map and the difference between them in percent of the rms of the first instead.
- `plot_map_aplpy.py`: Plot one final map with aplpy, blanking pixels without hits. The colour scale comes from the well-covered pixels (hits >= --scale-hits) so the noisy edge does not set the range.
- `plot_residual_curves.py`: Plot the CG relative residual |r|/|b| against the iteration from the logs of twores_ex.py --cg, one panel per group of runs (each --group is "title:label=log,label=log,...").
- `probe_band_gif.py`: GIF of how probe_band recovers the rows of a band's response matrix G = K^T O K, with O the normal matrix A^T N^-1 A (or the ACT analogue). Impulses further apart than the kept window are summed into one map and passed through O together, so one application of O gives one row of G for every impulse in the group. The groups are the ones color_groups gives probe_band.
- `ridge_test.py`: Test the block diagonal solve of WavSkyMap.solve_bands against PCG on a small M2-only problem.
- `sim_compare.py`: Compare twores_ex.py solutions of a simulation (--sim-sky) with the input sky: for each run and saved CG iteration, the error and the transfer (recovered over input) in Gaussian band-passes inside the footprint, split into the edge and the interior, and the central decrement.
- `sim_multiscale.py`: Two-resolution display maps (multiscale_map.py) of a simulation: truth, the simulated data and their difference, for one or more twores_ex runs on the simulated sky. Truth and data go through the same display steps, so the residual compares like with like: - truth: the simulated sky (make_sim_sky.py) at sub-pixel resolution where M2 has data, and outside the sky as a noiseless ACT sees it (ACT beam, then averaged over each ACT pixel); - data: the run's joint map where M2 has data, and outside a simulated ACT map, the same ACT view of the sky plus white noise from the real ACT inverse variance (as twores_ex.sim_act does on the mapmaking grid). For visualization only.
- `summary_figs.py`: Figures for the convergence summary page: the simulation solves against the truth per scale and iteration (PNG and a GIF of the large-scale maps), the slow edge modes from the Ritz analysis, and the effect of the unit fix.
- `tod_splits.py`: Jackknife splits of the MUSTANG-2 TODs for twores_ex.py --tod-list: the same TODs twores_ex would use (sorted, blacklist removed, the first --ntods-total), split in two halves by time (file order, which follows the observing sessions) and by mean elevation. Writes one text file of TOD paths per half and prints the split.
- `transfer_overlap.py`: Overlap of the M2 and ACT transfer functions and sensitivities inside the M2 footprint, per Fourier mode.
